Treat "internal clients" and "in-house customers" as UNCLEAR unless a later answer explicitly resolves who attended. Generic drinks alone do not establish alcohol. Preserve distinctions such as soft drinks, no alcohol, and non-alcoholic beer.
Your main job is the CLIENT CHECK: who attended?

- "CLIENT": the memo names a specific EXTERNAL organization AND it is clear they are a customer/client of ours. Use "high" only when both the name and the client relationship are clear.
- "INTERNAL": only our own employees attended. Use "high" only when the memo clearly says so (e.g. "team celebration", "our team offsite", "with coworkers", "internal").
- "UNCLEAR": an organization or group is mentioned but you cannot tell if they are a client (vendor, partner, supplier, recruiter, "contract" talks with an unclear relationship), OR the memo says "client" without naming one, OR you are otherwise unsure.
- If the memo says "client" but gives no name, set client_name null and attendee_confidence "low".
- "team" on its own is not decisive: "Beacon Inc team" is a client's team; "Team dinner" may be internal or not.

alcohol_mentioned = true if the memo mentions beer, wine, drinks, cocktails, spirits, a bar tab, etc.
Set city, nights, suggested_category to null; city_confidence "low".
